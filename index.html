02 / How the planner works

From dog name to paper kit

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and the kit prints on one sheet.

  1. Fill it in

    Dog name, age and the party date go on the top.

  2. Pick the fun

    Duration, guest count and two or three easy games.

  3. Print the kit

    One A4 sheet: run of show, guest list, checklists.

  4. Throw the party

    Put the bowl down, wave the banner, done.
